The existing Business Central and NAV artwork is reused for matching hero, profile, Skills and project technology labels. C/AL keeps its existing label and uses the NAV product mark because it is associated with that environment.

Small inline logos have empty alternative text because the adjacent visible product name supplies the accessible label. Existing certification product icons remain in place.
